VERIFICATION, WITH PRIVACY IN MIND

Tamper-Evident
Laboratory Records

RHU LabChain is designed to keep actual medical information in the secured clinical database. Blockchain will store verification hashes, internal record identifiers, timestamps, and important laboratory events.

That means a record’s integrity can be checked without placing personal information or full laboratory results on the blockchain.

A FOUNDATION OF TRUST

Privacy at the core.
Security at every step.

Our design commitments guide how RHU LabChain will handle, protect, and verify laboratory information. Access controls, audit logging, HTTPS, and tamper detection are planned capabilities, not claims of completed deployment.

  • Medical records remain in MySQL
  • Blockchain stores verification data only
  • Role-based user access
  • Audit logging
  • Secure HTTPS communication
  • Tamper detection
